### What is Cabine?

Cabine centralizes the operational benchmarks, calculation engines, and legal riders required for high-stakes multilingual conferences. From mathematical team-sizing quotas and ISO acoustic tolerances to multi-booth quoting and automated dispatch, Cabine eliminates spreadsheet errors and protects operational margins.

- **Fast Quoting**: Quote multi-booth, multi-day RFPs in 60 seconds with turn-taking rotation formulas.
- **Acoustic Compliance**: Verify mobile and built-in booth dimensions against ISO 4043 and ISO 2603 standards.
- **Signal & Relay Architecture**: Map pivot channels and monitor Dante / AES67 network latency cascades.
- **Zero-Friction Dispatch**: Dispatched interpreters receive tokenized mobile passcards with zero login or seat licensing fees.

### Standards & Compliance Specifications

Cabine implementations reference international conference engineering and acoustic standards:
- **ISO 4043**: Mobile simultaneous interpreting booths (acoustic insulation, silent ventilation `< 40 dB`, clear sightlines).
- **ISO 2603**: Built-in permanent conference booths.
- **ISO 20108**: Audio and video quality requirements for interpreting.
- **AES67 / Dante**: Low-latency multichannel digital audio transport over IP.
